The Doorman
Creators: Adam, Marco, Gabriel
Materials: Arduino Nano, ultrasonic distance sensor, blue LED, OLED display, and connecting wires
Year: 2026
The Doorman notices how close a person or object is to its sensor. It calls you CLEAR, NOTICED, HEAR, or UPCLOSE, depending on your distance. It then displays the name and distance on an OLED screen while a blue LED blinks whenever something is within 50 centimeters. When the area is clear, the light turns off. Our project demonstrates how technology makes judgments based on limited information. It refuses to identify who someone actually is because it only measures distance, not identity.
